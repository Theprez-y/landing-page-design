A modern publishing platform

Grow your audience and build your online brand

Introducing an extensible editor


Blogr features an exceedingly intuitive interface which lets you focus on one thing: creating content. The editor supports management of multiple blogs and allows easy manipulation of embeds such as images, videos, and Markdown. Extensibility with plugins and themes provide further ways to customize and extend your reach.



Robust content management


Flexible content management enables easy customization of your blog. Visualize drafts, schedule posts, get feedback and collaborate on content real-time.

Designed for the future

Free, open, simple


Blogr is a free and open source application backed by a large community of helpful developers. It supports features such as code syntax highlighting, RSS feeds, social media integration, third-party commenting tools, and works seamlessly with Google Analytics. The architecture is clean and is relatively easy to learn.



Powerful tooling


Batteries included. We built a simple and straightforward CLI tool that makes customization and deployment a breeze, but capable of producing even the most complicated sites.
